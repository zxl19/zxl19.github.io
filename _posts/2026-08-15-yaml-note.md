## YAML Hello World

1. YAML是一种人类可读的数据序列化语言，是YAML Ain't Markup Language的递归缩写；
2. YAML语法：

    - 整型：

        ```yaml
        canonical: 12345
        decimal: +12,345
        octal: 014
        hexadecimal: 0xC
        ```

    - 浮点型：

        ```yaml
        canonical: 1.23015e+3
        exponential: 12.3015e+02
        fixed: 1,230.15
        ```

    - 字符串：

        ```yaml
        string: foo
        doublequoted: "Double-quotes allow escape sequences.\u263A"
        singlequoted: 'Single quotes do not'
        ```

    - 杂项：

        ```yaml
        true: true
        false: false
        true-y: y
        false-n: n
        string: '12345'
        ```

    - 列表和字典：

        ```yaml
        dict: {a: 1, b: 2}
        list: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
        ```
